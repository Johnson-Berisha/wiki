## General

* Leaves decay much faster on trees after all the wood has been broken.
* Stonecutters deal half a heart of damage every 0.4 seconds to entities standing on them, accompanied by a saw sound effect.
* Players can build elevators to traverse between floors.
  * Drop an Echo Shard onto a shulker box, chest, or barrel to create an elevator block. Then do the same process to another container at the same X and Z coordinates, but different Y coordinates. You can now jump or sneak to go up or down respectively. The maximum vertical distance between elevator blocks is 64 blocks.
  * Echo Shards are permanently consumed upon setting up an elevator.
  * Player is required to be [**2** 🌟](/earth/gameplay/stars/#star-2) to use this feature.
* Players can transfer pet ownership to another player by leashing a tamed pet and right-clicking the target player.
  * Player is required to be [**5** 🌟](/earth/gameplay/stars/#star-5) to use this feature.
* Players can rotate redstone components and glazed terracotta blocks by left-clicking them with a lightning rod.
  * Sneaking while left-clicking reverses the rotation direction.
  * Extended pistons cannot be rotated.
  * Player is required to be [**12** 🌟](/earth/gameplay/stars/#star-12) to use this feature.
* Starvation is fully lethal on Normal difficulty for all players.
  * Additionally, staying stationary or AFK at zero hunger will strip your health regeneration and disable your ability to attack.

## Mobs

* Axolotls can breed when fed tropical fish items, not just tropical fish buckets.
* Axolotls no longer attack passive mobs. (They will still go for hostile mobs!)
* Polar bears can be bred by feeding them salmon.
* Shulkers can change colors by right-clicking them with a dye item.
* Villagers and Wandering Traders can be leashed with a Lead.
* Wither's health bar color can be changed by right-clicking it with a dye item.
* Right-clicking a custom-named mob with a Clock freezes or unfreezes its movement. Frozen mobs emit a snowflake particle aura.
  * Cannot be used on pets owned by other players.
* Feeding a Golden Apple to your own tamed pet instantly restores it to full health.
* Right-clicking a Parrot with Red, Blue, Green, Cyan, or Gray dye changes its plumage to match the corresponding variant.
* Right-clicking a Fox with a Red Tulip converts it into a Red Fox, while a White Tulip converts it into a Snow Fox.
* Right-clicking Cows, Pigs, Chickens, or Frogs with environmental items transforms their climate variant:
  * Packed Ice transforms them into Cold variants.
  * Podzol transforms them into Temperate variants.
  * Magma Cream transforms them into Warm variants.
* Over 30 passive mob species retaliate when attacked and have a 10% chance to spawn naturally aggressive (Chicken, Cow, Sheep, Pig, Rabbit, Goat, Armadillo, Camel, Sniffer, Horse, Donkey, Mule, Llama, Trader Llama, Panda, Polar Bear, Fox, Cat, Ocelot, Parrot, Turtle, Frog, Axolotl, Bat, Allay, Villager, Wandering Trader, Dolphin, Cod, Salmon, Pufferfish, Tropical Fish, Tadpole, and Strider).
* Rabbits have a 5% chance to spawn naturally as The Killer Bunny.
* Baby Zombies have a 20% chance to spawn riding an animal mount (Goats, Pandas, Foxes, Sheep, Cows, Pigs, Wolves, or baby Sniffers).
* Illusioners have a 15% chance to spawn in Abandoned Camp structures.
* Players with the **Amethyst** rank can ride any custom-named mob in Earth by right-clicking it.
  * Standard rideable mobs (such as saddled pigs or horses) still follow vanilla rules across all worlds.

## Mob Drops

* Turtles have a 35% chance to drop 1-2 sea pickles.
* Turtles have a 10% base chance to drop 1 to 2 turtle scutes, scaling up to 16% with Looting III.
* Silverfish drop 0 to 2 cracked stone bricks, increasing by 1 per Looting level.
* Silverfish drop 0 to 1 cobblestone.
* Silverfish have a 15% chance to drop 1 to 2 iron nuggets.

## Loot Tables

* Budding amethyst blocks can be picked up using tools with silk touch.
* Reinforced deepslate blocks can be picked up using tools with silk touch.
* Spawner blocks can be picked up using tools with silk touch.
  * Player is required to be [**1** 🌟](/earth/gameplay/stars/#star-1) to use this feature.
* Four secret paintings can be obtained if you get lucky.

## Tools

* Pickaxes can break light source blocks (glowstone, sea lanterns, etc.) faster.
* Pickaxes can break glass blocks and panes faster.
* Pickaxes can break reinforced deepslate blocks faster.
* Hoes can break froglights faster.
* Hoes can break cactus faster.
* Axes can break skulls and heads faster.
* When an item falls below 25% durability, you receive a loud alarm and a prominent warning showing the item type and remaining durability.
  * Alerts have a short personal cooldown (about 15 seconds).

## Enchantment Table

* Players can store lapis lazuli in the enchantment table indefinitely.
* Chiseled bookshelves can contribute to the total enchant power like normal bookshelves.
* Most transparent/non-full blocks (fences, carpets, banners, etc.) can be used in the enchant area without taking enchant power away.

## Nether

* Players may use crying obsidian when building a nether portal.
* Players can create non-rectangular nether portals.
* Player-made explosions have been disabled.
* Building blocks above the world height limit is not allowed.

## The End

* Player-made explosions have been disabled.
