## Requirements & Founding

Founding a nation unites multiple towns under a single territorial entity, removes claim buffers between member towns, and grants townblock and outpost bonuses.

- A town must have at least 5 residents to create a nation.
- The creation fee is `$125,000`, deducted from the town bank upon creation.
- The founding town becomes the capital city.
- Independent towns must have at least 3 residents to join an existing nation.

<Steps>
1. Verify the town meets the 5-resident threshold and has at least `$125,000` in the town bank. Funds can be deposited via `/t deposit [Amount]`.

2. Run `/n new [Nation Name]` within town boundaries. Spaces are automatically converted to underscores.

3. Invite towns to join using `/n invite [Town Name]`. Invited towns accept using `/accept`.

4. Deposit currency into the national bank using `/n deposit [Amount]` to cover ongoing daily upkeep.
</Steps>

:::caution[Disbandment]
Nations cannot hold debt. If the national bank cannot pay daily upkeep at `12:00` server time, the nation is deleted and member towns become independent. If a nation disbands due to resident counts dropping below tier requirements, the `$125,000` creation fee is refunded to the capital city bank.
:::

## Member Limits & Alliance Perks

- Nations can hold up to 10 member towns.
- Towns in the same nation ignore wilderness distance rules, allowing them to claim plots directly adjacent to one another.
- Residents can teleport to allied member towns and the capital city using `/n spawn [NationName]`.
