## Nation Levels

Nations progress through 10 distinct tiers based on the total collective resident count across all member towns. Advancing tiers grants bonus townblocks to all member towns, increases outpost limits, and unlocks monarch titles.

### Tier Benefits Breakdown

- Bonus town blocks are credited automatically as an additional claim allowance to every member town within the nation.
- Bonus outposts increase the total number of remote outposts each member town can establish.
- Member town upkeep surcharges apply a multiplier to each town's local plot upkeep for belonging to a higher-tier nation.
- Nation zone radius defines the wilderness buffer surrounding the capital city that restricts unauthorized building and interactions.

## Nation Ranks

Nation leaders can assign administrative, diplomatic, and financial roles to citizens across any member town using `/n rank add [Username] [Rank]`.

### Monarch / King

- Held by the mayor of the capital city.
- Holds full administrative control, including disbandment, capital relocation, taxation, setting the national spawn, and managing diplomatic declarations.

### Assistant

- Manages diplomacy and territorial expansion.
- Sends invitations to independent towns using `/n invite [TownName]`.
- Manages alliances and enemy declarations.
- Deposits funds into other town accounts.
- Assigns the Helper rank to citizens.

### Treasurer

- Manages national finances.
- Deposits to the nation bank and other accounts.
- Withdraws funds from the national treasury while inside the capital city.
- Views the nation bank transaction history and balance leaderboard via `/n baltop`.

### Helper

- Serves as a recruiter authorized to add towns to the nation using `/n add [TownName]`.

## Nation Titles & Surnames

Monarchs can assign custom titles and surnames to any citizen in the nation using `/n set title [Username] [Title]` and `/n set surname [Username] [Surname]`. 

Titles support standard color codes and MiniMessage hex formatting (`<#RRGGBB>`). Nation titles take visual precedence over local town titles in chat and status screens.
