## Town Levels

Towns advance through 12 progression tiers based on resident count. Higher levels grant larger claim limits, higher bonus block purchase caps, additional outpost slots, expanded debt capacity, and updated mayoral titles.

### Progression Dynamics

- Base claim limits represent the maximum territory chunks granted automatically by resident count.
- Bonus block caps specify the maximum additional chunks a town can purchase using `/t buy bonus [Amount]`.
- Outpost limits regulate the number of disconnected territories a town can claim via `/t claim outpost`.
- Debt caps determine the maximum negative treasury balance permitted during bankruptcy before the town falls into ruins.

## Town Ranks

Mayors can delegate responsibilities to residents using `/t rank add [Username] [Rank]`.

### Co-Mayor

- Holds near-identical authority to the Mayor over plots, claiming, settings, taxation, and finances.
- Can assign Builder, Banker, and Guard ranks.
- Cannot delete the town.
- Cannot create, join, or leave a nation.
- Cannot assign Assistant or Co-Mayor ranks.
- Stands first in line for mayoral succession and cannot be kicked from the town.

### Assistant

- Manages land and plots.
- Claims and unclaims town territory.
- Invites new residents using `/t invite [Username]`.
- Configures plot settings and toggles public town spawn status.
- Interacts with switches (doors, levers, buttons, containers) in town-owned communal land.
- Assigns the Noble cosmetic rank.
- Sends outlaws to jail plots.
- Cannot kick residents and cannot place or break blocks in town-owned land.
- Stands second in line for mayoral succession and cannot be kicked from the town.

### Banker

- Manages municipal accounts.
- Deposits and withdraws funds from the town bank.
- Views the town bank transaction history.
- Stands third in line for mayoral succession.

### Builder

- Holds unrestricted build, destroy, switch, and item use permissions across all town-owned communal property.
- Does not bypass permissions on privately owned resident plots.

### Guard

- Enforces town security.
- Jails and unjails players using town jail plots.
- Views the town prisoner list.
- Arrests active outlaws inside town borders.
- Cannot declare new outlaws via `/t outlaw add`.

## Town Titles & Surnames

Mayors and Co-Mayors can assign custom titles and surnames to residents using `/t set title [Username] [Title]` and `/t set surname [Username] [Surname]`. 

Titles appear before the player's username in chat and town status screens. Both standard Minecraft color formatting and MiniMessage hex color codes (`<#RRGGBB>`) are supported. If a resident's nation assigns a national title, the nation title overrides the town title.
