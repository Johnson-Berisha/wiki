In this page, we answer some of the most frequently asked questions about Vista Valley.

## What can I do on the server?

Vista Valley is an economy-driven sandbox with activities tailored for every tycoon playstyle:

- Establish a town, claim land, and build custom bases with zero risk of claims warfare or griefing.
- Dominate the marketplace by running player shops, selling materials via `/shop`, and managing large-scale town finances.
- Construct massive automated farms and industrial grinders to generate a steady stream of passive income.
- Compete in player-run PvP arenas and participate in interactive seasonal server events.
- Unlock powerful **custom enchantments** through capsules, specialized shops, or player trade to upgrade your gear far beyond vanilla limits.
- Launch global player warps to drive foot traffic, promote tourism, and scale your business operations.
- Connect with an active community using integrated proximity voice chat, joint building projects, and collaborative town systems.

## How many currencies are there on the server?

There are three distinct currencies on Vista Valley: **Money**, **Glims**, and **Gems**. Money serves as the primary currency for scaling your infrastructure and standard trade. Glims act as a play-to-earn engagement currency spent mainly in the `/glimshop`. Gems are the premium currency obtained by supporting the server via the online store.

## What is money used for?

[Money](/earth/economy/#money) is the lifeblood of the server's economy. It is required to promote your personal Star ranks, expand your town or nation infrastructure, purchase bulk resources from the server `/shop`, and trade with other players.

## How do I make money?

You can generate [money](/earth/economy/#money) by claiming rewards from open capsules, selling automated farm outputs directly to the server `/shop`, or supplying rare goods to other players through the marketplace.

## What is the best way to make money?

Building large-scale automated farms that produce high volumes of sellable materials is the most effective long-term strategy for wealth accumulation. A classic entry-level setup is a massive cactus farm, which yields consistent, passive revenue through `/shop` sales.

## What is an easy way to make money?

A beginner-friendly method is to hang out or go AFK at the Glim Hub to accumulate Glims, which can then be sold directly to veteran players in exchange for quick start-up cash.

## What are Glims?

[Glims](/earth/economy/#glims) are an engagement-based currency awarded for active gameplay and server participation. They are primarily used to purchase high-tier items from Althea or inside the `/glimshop`.

## How do I earn Glims?

You can accumulate Glims by spending time inside the Glim Hub, voting for the server, holding a Glims Pass, opening dynamic capsules, or trading with other community members.

## What are Gems?

[Gems](/earth/economy/#gems) are the premium currency on Vista Valley. They are primarily obtained through the server store and can be exchanged for exclusive cosmetics, seasonal capsules, and unique decorative items.

## Will I lose items upon death?

No, item loss is completely disabled. Vista Valley utilizes a **keep inventory** and keep experience system. However, dying still impacts your finances by deducting a small percentage of money from your personal balance, your town bank, and your nation bank.

## Is there a way to get enchantments faster?

Yes. You can visit established player-made villager hubs via public warps to secure standard vanilla books efficiently. Higher-tier **custom enchantments** are obtained through the `/archmage`, dynamic capsules, or direct player trade. For a full breakdown, check out the [custom enchantments](/earth/gameplay/custom-enchantments/) documentation.

## How do I use Warps?

You can access the [Warps](/earth/gameplay/warps/) system to teleport instantly to global infrastructure projects, server hubs, and player-owned marketplaces across the Earth map. 

## Is Voice Chat mod supported?

Yes, the server fully supports the [Simple Voice Chat](/earth/social/simple-voice-chat/) mod to allow for natural, immersive, 3D proximity communication with nearby players.

## What are Wraps?

[Wraps](/earth/fluff/wraps/) are custom decorative cosmetic skins applied to your tools and weapons to change their appearance. You can review the dedicated Wraps page to see the current catalog of available designs and unlock requirements.
